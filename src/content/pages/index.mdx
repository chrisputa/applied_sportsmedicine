## Aims and scope

This lecture series addresses the in-depth, systematic understanding of applied sports medicine aspects of health and performance. It is focussed on application of selected basics of aerobic performance and cardiorespiratory fitness in healthy healthy individuals, competitive athletes, and patients for exercise prescriptions.

## Interactive Learning Tools

Interactive tools are an integral part of the course and serve as essential learning resources. Interactive [dashboards](/dashboards/) are used throughout to explore and visualise key concepts. The <a href="https://spomed.svwiswp05.uni-jena.de/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Immunenotes app server</a> hosts a range of apps as essential learning tools, with accompanying [tutorials](/tutorials/) available here.
